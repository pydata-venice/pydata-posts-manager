# long

Next #PyData Venice Meetup 🚀🚀🚀

🕖 We’ll meet #InPresence on Thursday, December 17 at 7:00 PM.
🏠 We’ll be hosted by Anda Venice Hostel in the Medusa Room and, for those who cannot join us, it will also be on YouTube in #streaming.

👥 We’ll have two interesting sessions, in English 🇬🇧:
🗣 Varun Joshi will explain why #duckDB is fast: columnar storage, vectorized execution, and a query planner that differs from a row-oriented #database. Through benchmarks against pandas and PySpark up to 1B rows, we’ll see where each tool belongs in a modern #DataAnalytics stack.
🗣 Alessandra Bilardi will present a personal project born from a simple question: how far can a #SLM go at #coding ? From building the #FineTuning #dataset to putting the model to work, we'll see what it takes to teach a small model a real coding task, and where its limits are.

👥 And afterwards, for those who want, we’ll stay at Anda Venice Hostel for (🍸 | 🍺) & (🍕 | 🍔) and #networking until late‼️
