# long

Next #PyData Venice Meetup 🚀🚀🚀

🕖 We’ll meet #InPresence on Thursday, June 18 at 7:00 PM.
🏠 We’ll be hosted by Anda Venice Hostel in the Medusa Room and, for those who cannot join us, it will also be on YouTube in #streaming.

👥 We’ll have two interesting sessions one in Italian 🇮🇹 and one in English 🇬🇧:
🗣 Fabio Lamanna will present an analysis of the real impact of "Bologna #City30", the 30 km/h limit, using #FloatingCarData (#FCD) with #QGIS and #Python to compare pre/post driving speeds and measure how much the city respects the new limit.
🗣 Claudio Giancaterino will show a #MultiAgent system that turns car damage photos into an insurance claim assessment in minutes, where an Orchestrator coordinates a Vision Agent (#GPT-5.2), OEM/Aftermarket Cost Estimation Agents (#Perplexity), and a Shop Finder Agent, each running a transparent #ReAct reasoning loop to return damage severity, comparative repair costs, and recommended shops.

👥 And afterwards, for those who want, we’ll stay at Anda Venice Hostel for (🍸 | 🍺) & (🍕 | 🍔) and #networking until late‼️
