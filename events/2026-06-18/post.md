# long

The latest #PyDataVenice event took place last week 🚀🚀🚀

🎤 A particularly interesting evening: two talks both starring cars, made inclusive by our first #realtime #SpeechToSpeech system, so everyone could follow in their own language.

🚗 We warmed up with an analysis of geospatial data on Bologna's traffic, sparking a lively debate with the audience, who suggested how it could be taken further

🧩 We continued with an agentic system that, starting from a car's damage, returns damage severity, comparative repair costs, and recommended shops

🍻 Despite the heat, many stayed for #networking: rich discussion all together over a beer offered by Claudio !!!

🎦 Missed the #meetup? No worries, you can watch the live stream recording on #YouTube {url}

♥️ Thanks to both speakers, Fabio Lamanna and Claudio Giancaterino, for sharing their experience and expertise with us.
And a big #ThankYou to Anda Venice Hostel for hosting us in the Medusa room! 🏛️
